Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information in ReMeDi IMS.

Zero Trust Architecture · Firebase Secured
Last updated: August 2026  ·  Effective immediately upon app use

1. Overview

DiReMi IMS ("the App") is an Intelligent Inventory Management System developed for motor shops and small-to-medium enterprises. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use our Android application.

By registering and using ReMeDi IMS, you agree to the terms described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the application.

2. Data We Collect

We collect only the minimum data necessary to operate the system:

  • Account Information — Full name, email address, and encrypted password upon registration
  • Role & Branch Data — Your assigned role (Manager, Cashier, Stock Person) and branch affiliation
  • Inventory Records — Product names, SKUs, prices, and stock quantities you enter into the system
  • Transaction Records — Point-of-sale transactions including items sold, quantities, and totals
  • Device Camera — Used solely for barcode scanning via ZXing. No images are captured or stored
  • Usage Metadata — Timestamps of key actions for audit logging purposes under ZTA compliance

3. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to operate the ReMeDi IMS application:

  • Authenticating your identity and managing your session
  • Enforcing role-based access control — you only see data relevant to your role and branch
  • Generating sales reports and AI-powered forecasts for your branch
  • Maintaining audit logs of sensitive actions as required by Zero Trust Architecture
  • Sending one-time password (OTP) emails for two-factor authentication

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

4. Security & Zero Trust Architecture

ReMeDi IMS is built on Zero Trust Architecture (ZTA) principles — every access request is verified regardless of origin.

Security Measures in Place
  • Firebase Authentication with encrypted credential storage
  • Email OTP two-factor authentication on every login
  • reCAPTCHA v2 verification on account registration
  • Step-up password re-authentication before sensitive operations
  • Firestore security rules enforcing role and branch-level data isolation
  • All data transmitted over HTTPS/TLS encrypted connections
  • On-device AI inference — no sales data leaves your device for predictions

5. Third-Party Services

ReMeDi IMS integrates the following third-party services. Each has its own privacy policy:

Firebase Authentication Firebase Firestore Google reCAPTCHA v2 ZXing Barcode Scanner TensorFlow Lite Gmail SMTP (OTP only)

Firebase services are governed by Google's Privacy Policy. We use Firebase solely for authentication and data storage — no Firebase Analytics or Ads services are enabled.

6. Data Retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. Specifically:

  • Account data is retained until you request deletion
  • Transaction records are retained indefinitely for reporting purposes within your branch
  • OTP codes expire after 5 minutes and are never stored in our database
  • Audit logs are retained for security and compliance purposes

7. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access — Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction — Request correction of inaccurate personal data
  • Deletion — Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Portability — Request an export of your data in a readable format

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the information below.

8. Children's Privacy

ReMeDi IMS is intended for business use by adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under the age of 13. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of ReMeDi IMS after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
